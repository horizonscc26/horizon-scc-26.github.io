0 3   /   GUIDELINES

Before you submit.

Everything participants need to know before submitting their research.

01

Eligibility

Open to eligible undergraduate students as primary authors, with Postgraduate students, Academicians and Research Scholars eligible to serve as co-authors.

02

Abstract

Participants must submit their research abstract during registration.

03

Presentation

Selected participants will receive their full paper presentation instructions from the organising committee.

04

Formatting

Abstracts must be 250–300 words and include 4–5 keywords. They must follow the prescribed formatting guidelines: Times New Roman, 12.5 pt font, double spacing, and APA 7th Edition referencing.

The abstract submission should be structured as follows: Page 1: Title of the Paper; Author and Co-author(s) Name, Designation, and E-mail ID. Page 2: Abstract and Keywords.

05

Submission

All information submitted must be accurate and complete.
